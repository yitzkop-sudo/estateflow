FAQ

Questions, answered

What is EstateFlow?

EstateFlow is an all-in-one property management app for landlords and property managers. Manage unlimited properties, assign tenants, collect rent by card, track utilities, handle maintenance and watch your whole portfolio in the Insights dashboard — with a separate portal website so your tenants can pay and submit requests themselves.

Who uses the tenant portal website?

Your tenants. It's included with the Professional plan: each tenant signs in with their own account, sees their rent and due day, pays by card through Stripe, reviews payment history and submits maintenance requests — and every payment shows up in your app instantly.

How does utility auto-sync work?

On Starter or Professional, tap Connect Utility Provider on a property, pay $20 per meter per month, sign in to your provider through a secure UtilityAPI form, and the bill pulls itself in — amount, provider and due day. It refreshes automatically every month, and up to 12 months of history is backfilled when the meter first links. Availability depends on your utility provider; Electric, Water, Gas, Oil, Sewer and Trash meters are supported.

What does the $20/meter fee cover?

It covers your meter's data costs with UtilityAPI — $3 per meter per month to keep bills refreshing, plus a one-time $12 history backfill when the meter is first connected — as well as updates and support. There are no other per-meter charges.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

Yes — upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime. If you cancel auto-sync, meters unlink gracefully and you can always keep entering utilities manually for free.

Is my data secure?

Accounts are protected by Firebase Authentication, payments are processed by Stripe (card details never touch EstateFlow), and utility provider credentials are handled entirely by UtilityAPI's secure forms — EstateFlow never sees your provider password.
