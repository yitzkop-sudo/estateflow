Privacy Policy

Provalta · Last updated October 2026

Provalta ("we", "our") is a property-management app. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Data you provide

  • Account: name, email address and login credentials (including Google sign-in profile info: name, email, profile photo).
  • Properties & tenancy: property details, tenant names, rent amounts, maintenance records and notes you enter.
  • Utilities: utility amounts, providers and due dates, including bills synced through providers you authorize.
  • Billing: subscription and payment details, processed securely by our payment provider (Stripe). We never store full card numbers.

How we use it

To operate your account, sync authorized utility data, process subscriptions, send due-date reminders, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal data.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In — authentication (subject to Google's Privacy Policy).
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Utility data network — pulls bills only from providers you explicitly authorize; access stops when your subscription ends.
  • Firebase (Google Cloud) — secure data hosting and authentication.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. To request export or deletion of your data, contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.
